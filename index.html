Spansh data. Your Google Sheet.

The app queries Spansh for game data and uses the Google Sheets API to read scan settings and write results to your connected spreadsheet. Google OAuth lets you authorize that connection in your browser.

Google Sheets is an intentional part of the tool: sort and filter results, add formulas, change formatting, create columns and tabs, or copy data for your own analysis without changing the program.

Open Template opens the official ED Hotspots & Landables Finder template for copying. Connect / Change Sheet connects your copy or another Google Spreadsheet; Open My Sheet opens the connected file. Missing required tabs are added from the template; existing tabs are not replaced by template copies. The main sheet's functional structure can be repaired.

Use Repair Sheet, Restore Official Style and Save Current Style to maintain or customize your sheet. The Minerals Table and Volcanism Table are editable references.

Your data and authorization

The app keeps its Google token and configuration on your computer. System names and search criteria are sent to Spansh to perform searches. Read the Privacy Policy for the exact Google permissions, local files and ways to revoke access.
