Community Deposits

A shared database for planetary mining discoveries.

Surface locations are much more useful when they do not stay on one commander's PC. Community Deposits is designed to build a searchable collection of player-reported planetary deposits over time.

Discover→Bookmark→Synchronize→Share→Search

The database is community-reported and still growing. Missing data does not mean a deposit is absent; every contribution improves future searches.

RhinoSpotter integration

Bookmark in-game. Sync when you choose.

RhinoSpotter, developed independently by Fumlop, is an EDMC plugin for recording planetary surface-mining locations while you play.

ED Hotspots Finder can detect RhinoSpotter's local bookmark database and synchronize compatible bookmarks with Community Deposits. The integration is optional, user-triggered and reads the RhinoSpotter SQLite database in read-only mode.

Current integration tested with RhinoSpotter 4.4.3. Legacy JSON cards remain supported as a fallback.
